The problem

Healthcare information changes between appointments.

Patients forget details, struggle with long forms, and describe the same concern differently each time. Doctors spend valuable time reconstructing history instead of reviewing it.

Before

  • Long intake forms
  • Phone calls to relay updates
  • Scattered notes across visits
  • Repeated questions
  • Information overload at check-in

With CareTraceAI

  • Natural conversation, anytime
  • Structured, dated information
  • A continuous timeline
  • An instant "What changed?" view
  • A concise, doctor-ready brief
How it works

From conversation to clinical clarity

03 — Track

Every update is dated, kept, and never silently overwritten.

04 — Prepare

Doctor gets a brief

A concise, patient-reported summary — ready before the visit.

Trust & safety

Designed to assist clinicians, not replace them.

No diagnosis

CareTraceAI does not diagnose medical conditions. It organizes what the patient reports.

No prescribing

CareTraceAI never recommends or prescribes medication.

Clinician in control

All clinical decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals.
